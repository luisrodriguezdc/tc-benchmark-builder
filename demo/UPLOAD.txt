Tiiny Host upload

Upload demo/tc-benchmark-builder-demo.zip.
index.html is at the root of the zip, which is what Tiiny Host expects.

You can also upload demo/index.html by itself.

The page is a demo. Annotations stay in the browser. They are not written to the live database.
No login, Supabase, or Python server is required.

Open demo/index.html in a browser, or serve the demo folder:

    python3 -m http.server 8765

Then visit http://127.0.0.1:8765/

To rebuild after the pilot CSV changes:

    python3 demo/build_demo.py
